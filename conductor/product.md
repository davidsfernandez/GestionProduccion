# Gestion Produccion

## Product Vision
Gestion Produccion is a comprehensive production management system tailored for factory operations. Its primary objective is end-to-end production tracking, providing real-time visibility into stages, tasks, and operational efficiency.

## Target Audience
The system serves a wide range of roles within the organization:
- **Floor Workers & Managers:** Operators interacting with active tasks and floor managers overseeing daily operations.
- **QA & Operations Directors:** Quality Assurance personnel inspecting products, logging defects, and directors tracking high-level metrics.
- **Admins & Executives:** Administrators and Executives configuring bonus rules, managing teams, and reviewing comprehensive reports.

## Core Features
- **Order Lifecycle Management:** Detailed tracking of production orders through various operational stages.
- **Team Management:** Assigning tasks and closely monitoring sewing team performance.
- **Dashboards & Reports:** Providing real-time TV dashboard displays and robust reporting tools for performance tracking.

## User Experience (UX) Priorities
The application aims to deliver a versatile and robust experience combining:
1. Floor-Ready UI optimized for tablets and touchscreens on the factory floor.
2. Fast, data-dense interfaces tailored for back-office management.
3. Real-time visibility with live updates and minimal manual refreshes.
