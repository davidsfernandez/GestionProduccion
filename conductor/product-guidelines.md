# Product Guidelines

## Visual Branding & Tone
- **Corporate, Functional & Sleek:** The visual tone should strike a balance between a clean, data-dense corporate look for back-office tasks and a modern, engaging, and polished interface.

## User Experience (UX) Principles
- **Role-Based Adaptive UI:** The interface must seamlessly adapt based on the logged-in user's role:
  - **Factory Floor (Touch-First):** Large touch targets, minimal typing, and scanning-ready interactions for operators and floor managers.
  - **Back-Office (Data-Entry):** Keyboard shortcuts, dense data grids, and complex filtering capabilities for administrators and executives.

## Feedback & Error Handling
- **Subtle Notifications:** Use unobtrusive toast messages for general feedback and inline form validation to maintain a smooth user workflow without unnecessary blocking.

## Language & Terminology
- **Codebase / Internal:** The codebase, domain models, variables, and technical documentation must be maintained exclusively in English.
- **User Interface:** All user-facing text, labels, and messages must be strictly in Brazilian Portuguese (Português do Brasil).
